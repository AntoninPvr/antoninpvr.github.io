                                                                                                            dev-board-enclosures
                                                                                                            Simple enclosures for dev boards
                                                                                                            no-release

                                                                                                            Overview

                                                                                                            Simple enclosure for STM32F411 It is designed to be 3D printed.

                                                                                                            Parts

                                                                                                            • stm32f411_top.STL Top part of the enclosure
                                                                                                            • stm32f411_bottom.STL Bottom part of the enclosure

                                                                                                            Assembly

                                                                                                            Provided 3mf file is tested and ready to print on Prusa MK3 . Parts are hold together by friction. No screws are needed.
